How to Assemble Your Care Package

  1. Choose a Theme: Pick colors or a mood for your package.
  2. Select Items: Add snacks, small gifts, and stationary.
  3. Write a Personal Note: Include a heartfelt message.
  4. Arrange Neatly: Layer items for presentation.
  5. Seal & Send: Secure your box and ship!
